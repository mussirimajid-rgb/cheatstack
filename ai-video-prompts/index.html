15 AI Image & Video Prompts for Faceless YouTube Shorts

Every prompt here is built from blocks: a CHARACTER block and a STYLE block you copy word for word, plus one SCENE line you rewrite. That's how one character survives eight different shots. These are the exact templates from my own videos.

01–03 lock the character, 04–12 set the style, 13–15 are image-to-video motion prompts. Build every prompt as CHARACTER + STYLE + one SCENE line.

01 Mascot lock

Use the attached reference image for the character [NAME]: keep the exact [body shape], [skin/colour], [eyes], [eyebrows], [hair], [top] and [bottoms]. Vertical 9:16, keep the top fifth simple for captions. No text, no letters, no watermark, no logo.

02 Faceless human

A person seen only from behind or from the shoulders down, hands visible, wearing a plain [colour] hoodie, face never shown. Vertical 9:16, top fifth simple for captions. No text, no watermark.

03 Talking object

A [object] with small expressive cartoon eyes and a tiny mouth, otherwise realistic materials and proportions, the same eyes and colours in every shot. Vertical 9:16, top fifth simple for captions. No text, no logo.

04 Cinematic 3D

High-end stylized 3D animated feature-film still. Rich saturated colors and high contrast. Dramatic cinematic lighting: strong cold electric-blue rim light from behind and a warm orange key light on the face. Deep moody environment colors so the character pops. Wide-angle lens, close so the character fills about half the frame. Small particles caught mid-air in the light, glossy highlights, shallow depth of field.

05 X-ray cutaway

Split-view educational cutaway in a clean 3D medical-animation look. Above the dividing line the character looks normal; below it the body is semi-transparent like an x-ray, revealing the relevant organ glowing hot red or orange, with neon cyan lines showing what is happening inside. Dark navy background, neon cyan and red highlights, high contrast, close framing.

06 Paper cutout

Layered paper-cutout pop-up storybook diorama, visible paper edges and soft shadows between layers, warm tungsten light, faceless paper figures, textured card stock, shallow depth of field.

07 Claymation

Squishy claymation stop-motion look, visible fingerprints and tool marks in the clay, soft studio lighting, saturated candy colors, miniature handmade set.

08 Macro nature

Hyper-detailed macro nature photography look, extreme close-up, dewdrops, razor-thin focus plane, dark natural background, rim-lit edges, frozen slow-motion moment.

09 Epic space

Epic cinematic space CGI, enormous sense of scale, deep black space with glowing atmosphere edges, volumetric light rays, tiny details for scale, IMAX-style composition.

10 Product macro

Premium commercial product shot, the [product] on a dark reflective surface, single hard key light raking across the texture, soft fill, floating dust particles in the beam, macro lens, deep shadows, luxury ad look. No text, no logo.

11 Bold 2D cartoon

Bright flat 2D cartoon with thick black outlines, simple shapes, punchy primary colors, comic-book action lines, clean white or single-color background.

12 Desk B-roll

Top-down view of a minimal dark desk with a laptop showing a blurred editing interface, a coffee mug, soft window light from the left, muted dark tones with one lime-green accent object. No readable text on screen.

13 Impact reveal

Animate this exact image. Fast push-in toward the character's face, particles burst outward on impact, a tiny camera shake at the peak. Keep the character, colors and style exactly as in the image. No text.

14 Calm explainer

Animate this exact image. Slow orbit around the subject, the glowing lines pulse gently and travel along their paths, everything else stays still. Keep the character, colors and style exactly as in the image. No text.

15 Reaction loop

Animate this exact image. The character blinks, does a small double-take and freezes in a funny expression; the camera holds still. Keep the character, colors and style exactly as in the image. No text.
