25 YouTube Shorts Hooks That Stop the Scroll (Faceless Channels)

A hook only works if the picture is already moving. Put the action in second one and say one of these over it, then tell a tiny story where something goes wrong. The fact lands as the twist.

  1. This is the worst thing you can do when ___.
  2. You're doing ___ wrong. Here's why.
  3. Never ___. Here's what happens.
  4. Here's what happens inside your body when ___.
  5. Nobody tells you this about ___.
  6. This looks normal. It isn't.
  7. He tried to ___. Bad idea.
  8. You have 10 seconds to ___.
  9. What if ___ disappeared tomorrow?
  10. Scientists still can't explain ___.
  11. Stop scrolling if you ___.
  12. This one costs $1. This one costs $1,000.
  13. Why does ___? The answer is gross.
  14. The ___ rule nobody follows.
  15. I made this with AI in 10 minutes.
  16. Don't ___ until you watch this.
  17. This is how ___ actually works.
  18. Everyone gets this wrong about ___.
  19. Watch what happens when ___.
  20. This tiny mistake ruined ___.
  21. Rate this ___ from 1 to 10.
  22. It's 3 AM and you hear ___.
  23. Here's the fastest way to ___.
  24. Same ___, two prices. Spot the difference.
  25. Wait for the ending.
