30 ChatGPT Prompts That Save Hours (Copy & Paste)

Thirty prompts that do real work, grouped by job. Replace the [brackets] with your own details. Always check facts, names and numbers in the answer, because AI models can make things up.

Study

01 Explain it simply

Explain [topic] like I'm 12, then give me a harder version for an exam, then 3 questions to test myself.

02 Notes to flashcards

Turn these notes into 20 question-and-answer flashcards. Keep each answer under 20 words: [paste notes]

03 Practice exam

Write a 10-question practice test on [topic] at [level]. Show the answers only after I reply.

04 Find my gaps

Ask me one question at a time about [topic] until you find what I don't understand, then teach me that part.

05 Study plan

I have [X] days until my [subject] exam and [Y] hours a day. Make a day-by-day plan with review days.

06 Summarise a reading

Summarise this in 5 bullet points, then list the 3 ideas most likely to be tested: [paste text]

Writing

07 Fix my writing

Fix the grammar and make this clearer without changing my voice. List what you changed: [paste text]

08 Make it shorter

Cut this to half the length. Keep every fact: [paste text]

09 Better titles

Give me 10 titles for [topic], each under 60 characters. Mix curiosity, numbers and how-to.

10 Outline first

Write an outline for a [length] article on [topic] for [audience]. Headings and one line per section.

11 Change the tone

Rewrite this in a [friendly / formal / confident] tone for [audience]: [paste text]

12 Hook + ending

Write 5 opening lines and 3 closing lines for this piece: [paste text]

Work

13 Polite email

Write a short, polite email to [person] about [situation]. I need [outcome]. Under 120 words.

14 Meeting summary

Turn these meeting notes into decisions, action items with owners, and open questions: [paste notes]

15 Plan a project

Break [project] into steps with time estimates and the order to do them in. Flag the risky parts.

16 Spreadsheet formula

Write a [Excel / Google Sheets] formula that [what you want]. My data is in columns [A–D]. Explain it.

17 Prioritise

Here's my to-do list. Sort it by urgent and important, and tell me what to drop: [paste list]

18 Hard conversation

Help me prepare for a conversation with [person] about [issue]. Give me an opener and 3 likely replies.

Career

19 Resume bullets

Rewrite these job duties as resume bullets that start with a verb and show results: [paste duties]

20 Tailor to a job

Compare my resume to this job ad. List missing keywords and suggest edits: [paste both]

21 Cover letter

Write a 200-word cover letter for [role] at [company] using these 3 achievements: [list]

22 Mock interview

Interview me for [role]. One question at a time. Score each answer and tell me how to improve it.

23 Negotiate salary

I got an offer of [amount] for [role] in [city]. Write a polite counter-offer email.

24 LinkedIn about

Write a LinkedIn About section for a [role] who [what you do]. First person, under 150 words.

Everyday

25 Meal plan

Make a 7-day dinner plan for [number] people, under [budget], with a shopping list by aisle.

26 Budget check

Here are my monthly costs. Find 5 realistic ways to save [amount] a month: [paste list]

27 Trip planner

Plan [days] days in [city] for someone who likes [interests]. Group places by area to save travel time.

28 Workout plan

Make a [days]-day weekly workout plan for a beginner with [equipment], about [minutes] minutes a day.

29 Decide faster

I'm choosing between [A] and [B]. Ask me 5 questions, then give me a recommendation and why.

30 Learn a skill

Make a 30-day plan to learn [skill] in [minutes] a day, with one small project each week.
