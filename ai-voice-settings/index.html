ElevenLabs Voice Settings Cheat Sheet for Faceless YouTube Narration

A flat AI voice kills retention faster than bad visuals. These are my starting presets; change one slider at a time from here. They're my own settings, not official recommendations.

PresetStabilitySimilarityStyle
Calm documentary60%80%10%
Energetic hook35%75%40%
Storyteller45%80%30%
Deadpan narrator70%85%0%

Lower stability = more emotion but more random takes. Higher style = more drama but slower and occasionally odd.

Eleven v3: tags instead of sliders

Eleven v3: steer with one tag per sentence instead of sliders. Put the tag right before the words it should affect.

[urgent][deadpan][whispers][sighs][laughs][excited][curious]
[urgent] So you've fallen through the ice.
[deadpan] Here's the thing. The cold won't get you first.
[whispers] Panic will.

Voice cloning rule: only clone your own voice, or one you have written permission to use.
